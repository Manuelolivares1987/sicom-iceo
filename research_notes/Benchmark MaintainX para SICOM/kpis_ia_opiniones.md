## 1. Built-in reports and dashboards (which KPIs, custom dashboards, scheduled reports)

### Takeaway
MaintainX organizes reporting in four layers: (a) fixed standard reports (Work Orders, Asset Health, Reporting Details), (b) Report Builder (custom reports over datasets, with templates and natural-language prompting), (c) custom dashboards that combine reports, and (d) an activity log. Reliability KPIs (MTTR, MTBF, availability, downtime, planned vs unplanned) are calculated **from the asset's Online/Offline status transitions**, and admins set Great/Okay/Bad benchmarks for them. The more powerful parts (Report Builder, unlimited dashboards, Asset Health Insights, multi-site reporting) are Enterprise-only. Reporting runs only on the web, not in the mobile app.

### Cited Findings
- The Reporting module has: standard reports (Work Orders, Asset Health, Reporting Details) that can't be edited beyond filters and date range; Recent Activity, a log of actions for auditing; Report Builder, custom reports that combine datasets with aggregations, calculations and a chosen visualization, built from scratch, from a template or by AI prompt; and custom dashboards that combine reports — [Help: About Reporting](https://help.getmaintainx.com/about-reporting)
- Work Orders KPIs: completion rate, MTTR, MTBF, on-time performance, labor hours and workload distribution. Asset Health KPIs: availability, downtime and performance and reliability trends, **based on Online/Offline status transitions** — [Help: About Reporting](https://help.getmaintainx.com/about-reporting)
- Asset Health (Mar 2024, introduced as "Asset Health Insights" in Dec 2023) added charts for Total Downtime, MTBF, MTTR and Availability Over Time. Total downtime is broken into **planned vs unplanned**. Each chart has a tooltip explaining how it is calculated. Admins set **"Great / Okay / Bad" benchmarks** for MTTR, MTBF, availability and % planned downtime. Taking a parent asset offline also takes its sub-assets offline. Availability is presented as one component of OEE — [MaintainX blog, Mar 2024](https://www.getmaintainx.com/blog/introducing-new-asset-health-reliability-insights-to-increase-productivity)
- Dashboards: combine reports, monitor KPIs, share views and build executive summaries. They can be filtered by location, team or technician — [Help: About Reporting](https://help.getmaintainx.com/about-reporting), [Blog custom dashboards](https://www.getmaintainx.com/blog/feature-spotlight-driving-continuous-improvement-with-custom-dashboards)
- Export and distribution: PDF snapshot of the current view (Work Orders, Asset Health, Reporting Details, Dashboards), saved to a "Download Center". CSV export of data. **"Share Externally"** creates a read-only link to a dashboard. **"Scheduled Export"** sets up recurring data exports. The help page **does not describe report delivery by email**. Global (multi-org) dashboards can't be exported or shared externally — [Help: About Reporting](https://help.getmaintainx.com/about-reporting)
- Reporting is available only in the web app, not mobile — [Help: About Reporting](https://help.getmaintainx.com/about-reporting)
- **Report Builder AI** (Apr 2026): the user types questions in plain language (e.g. "which parts are low on stock?") and gets custom visualizations over work order, parts, labor and asset data, with filtering, segmenting and drill-down, "without exporting to Power BI". Enterprise with Assist only — [What's new Apr 2026](https://getmaintainx.com/blog/whats-new-at-maintainx-april-2026). In Aug 2026 a "Report Builder AI Chat" was added (title of the help-center update; I did not read the content) — [Help Center Updates Aug 2026](https://help.getmaintainx.com/help-center-updates/august-2026)
- **Root Cause Analysis** (Apr 2026, Enterprise): structured problem → cause → corrective action records inside the workflow, with corrective actions tracked to closure and pre-built and custom reports of recurring failure patterns — [What's new Apr 2026](https://getmaintainx.com/blog/whats-new-at-maintainx-april-2026)
- Jul 2026: **Meters / Meter Readings** datasets in Report Builder, e.g. assets accumulating hours/km fastest, gaps in reading compliance by location, date or who entered the reading, and assets outside normal ranges. "Report Builder is available on the Enterprise plan" — [What's new Jul 2026](https://webflow.getmaintainx.com/blog/whats-new-at-maintainx-july-2026)
- Analytics history by plan: Basic gets 1 month of Advanced Analytics and 1 dashboard. Essential gets 3 months and 3 dashboards. Premium gets unlimited analytics. Enterprise adds Report Builder, unlimited dashboards, Asset Health Insights and multi-site reporting — [MaintainX Pricing](https://www.getmaintainx.com/pricing) (the 1/3/unlimited dashboard split comes from a table whose column mapping was ambiguous)
- MaintainX's own formulas: MTTR = total maintenance time / number of repairs; MTBF = total uptime / number of failures — [MaintainX blog mean-time KPIs](https://www.getmaintainx.com/blog/tracking-failure-metrics-with-a-cmms-mean-time-kpis)
- A competitor's criticism: CSV exports have fixed fields, filters reset each time you return to the page, and users ask for saved views and export templates — [Coast (competitor)](https://coastapp.com/blog/maintainx-review/)
- One aggregator scores MaintainX 8.8 on "Reporting and Dashboards" vs Limble's 9.0, and teams needing advanced BI turn to Power BI or Tableau via the API — summarized in search results from [Infraspeak (competitor)](https://infraspeak.com/en/compare/maintainx-review) and [Facilio (competitor)](https://facilio.com/blog/maintainx-cmms-review/)

### Inferences
- What is copyable for SICOM: (1) **configurable Great/Okay/Bad benchmarks per KPI**, so traffic-light colors come from thresholds the business sets rather than fixed values in code; (2) **a tooltip on every chart explaining the formula**, which reduces "why doesn't this match my Excel" disputes; (3) **planned vs unplanned downtime as a first-class split**; (4) **a read-only external link to a dashboard**, which SICOM already does in part with the client portal/QR; (5) a Root Cause Analysis module (problem→cause→action, closed) linked to NCs/OTs, which SICOM could connect to its existing NC flow.
- MaintainX bases availability on a manually toggled Online/Offline status. SICOM already has a stronger daily-status source (planner + GPS). That is an advantage: don't copy the manual toggle.
- The weak spot to avoid: reporting that is web-only and gated by plan, with no confirmed email delivery. SICOM can win with automatic emails (it already has a costs report and a papers report).
- Report Builder AI (natural language → chart) is the most distinctive feature. An in-house equivalent would be an LLM generating SQL over curated views, restricted by RLS.

### Gaps
- I found no documentation of specific **PM compliance**, **planned/reactive ratio by WO**, **backlog**, **wrench time** or **cost per asset** reports in the standard reports. Probably available through Report Builder (labor, parts and time & cost data on Premium+), but unconfirmed.
- I found no evidence of scheduled report emails (only "Scheduled Export" to the Download Center). It may exist; not verified.
- I did not see the exact availability formula (e.g. whether it excludes planned downtime).

## 2. AI: CoPilot / MaintainX Assist and other AI features

### Takeaway
MaintainX AI is an **assistant grounded in each asset's documents (uploaded manuals) plus "work order summaries"** generated when a WO is closed by voice or text. These summaries feed the asset's knowledge base, which grows with every closed WO. Around it sit passive ML features (anomaly detection in readings, smart time estimates), a procedure generator (from prompt, PDF, nameplate photo or voice), in-line suggestions inside the WO, cross-organization knowledge for identical assets, and natural language → report. Almost everything generative is **Enterprise-only**. MaintainX positions it as assistive, with a human always approving.

### Cited Findings
- Help-center AI overview: **MaintainX Assist** (generative; text or recorded prompts) searches asset documentation and MaintainX data, combines information across documents, and uses past interactions. It creates and improves WOs and procedures, translates SOPs, and supports troubleshooting/diagnosis and questions about history — [Help: AI Basics](https://help.getmaintainx.com/ai-basics)
- **Anomaly Detection** (passive ML): flags blank values, decimal-point errors, unexpected negatives/positives and values outside the historical range in procedure fields (Number, Amount, Meter Reading) and manual meter readings. It applies only if the field has appeared in **at least 3 prior WOs**. The user corrects, confirms or rejects the flag — [Help: AI Basics](https://help.getmaintainx.com/ai-basics)
- **Smart Time Estimates**: ML trained on past WOs (technician-entered time, estimated time, in-progress time by status, and total creation-to-completion time). It needs a match on **at least 2 attributes, one of which must be the Asset** (the other can be Procedure, Assignee or Category). It can apply automatically or with approval — [Help: AI Basics](https://help.getmaintainx.com/ai-basics)
- Limits MaintainX states itself: the AI makes no changes without user action, human review is always required, it needs enough history, and without context "the AI guesses". "MaintainX does not use your personal data to train its AI models." The model provider is not named — [Help: AI Basics](https://help.getmaintainx.com/ai-basics)
- **Closing a WO with Assist**: when a WO is set to Done, a "Work Order Summary" dialog opens. The technician types or records a voice note (**max 2 minutes**). Assist transcribes it, the user can edit or re-record, and Assist then generates a **structured summary** stored in the WO's Summary field and in comments, and **attached to the asset's internal knowledge base**. If the notes lack detail it writes "N/A" instead of inventing. Audio is discarded after summarizing. Summaries are shared with the whole organization. Enterprise admins can delete summaries from the asset's sources (Ask MX Assist > Sources > Work order summaries), and Assist then stops using them. Enterprise; Full User or Admin roles — [Help: Complete a WO with Assist](https://help.getmaintainx.com/complete-a-work-order-with-maintainx-assist)
- Jul 2026: summaries can now be **typed** as well as recorded by voice. They still feed the knowledge base — [What's new Jul 2026](https://webflow.getmaintainx.com/blog/whats-new-at-maintainx-july-2026)
- **CoPilot Shared Assets** (Jul 2026, Enterprise with CoPilot): links identical assets across different MaintainX organizations. While working on one, CoPilot uses the other site's **WO summaries and manuals** — [What's new Jul 2026](https://webflow.getmaintainx.com/blog/whats-new-at-maintainx-july-2026)
- Assist "trained on your manuals, your work history". Typical question: "What's the shutdown procedure for this asset?", answered from OEM manuals and past WOs. Q2 2025: **identify equipment problems and recommend solutions from a photo**, and generate work summaries — [Blog CoPilot upgrade](https://webflow.getmaintainx.com/blog/maintainx-copilot-upgrade), [Help: Identify Asset Issues](https://help.getmaintainx.com/identify-asset-issues)
- **Work Order Suggestions** (late 2025): proactive in-line recommendations for the technician inside the WO. Enterprise — [Blog smarter WOs with suggestions](https://webflow.getmaintainx.com/blog/smarter-work-orders-with-copilot-suggestions) (search snippet)
- **AI procedure generator**: from a prompt or reference file (May 2026). In mobile, from **photos of the asset or nameplate and/or a voice prompt**. An Assist answer can be turned into a procedure ("Generate Procedure"). "AI Procedure Recommendations" is Enterprise with CoPilot — [Help: Create a procedure with AI](https://help.getmaintainx.com/create-a-procedure-with-AI), [Help: Procedure from Assist answer](https://help.getmaintainx.com/create-a-procedure-from-a-maintainx-assist-answer), [What's new May 2026](https://webflow.getmaintainx.com/blog/whats-new-at-maintainx-may-2026)
- AI translation of category names (LLM), with manual review. Manual translations are never overwritten — [Help: AI Basics](https://help.getmaintainx.com/ai-basics)
- Predictive / condition-based maintenance is handled through integrations, not in-house AI: KCF Technologies and Augury (Apr 2026) **automatically create a WO** with asset details, recommended steps and an owner when they detect an anomaly, fault or threshold event (Enterprise). OT connectors: Ignition 8.3 and custom MQTT for usage- and condition-based maintenance (Jul 2026) — [What's new Apr 2026](https://getmaintainx.com/blog/whats-new-at-maintainx-april-2026), [What's new Jul 2026](https://webflow.getmaintainx.com/blog/whats-new-at-maintainx-july-2026)
- Plan: Assist and Report Builder are listed under Enterprise. The comparison table says "AI features: Limited" for the other tiers — [MaintainX Pricing](https://www.getmaintainx.com/pricing). Assist WO features are not available on Basic, Essential or Premium — [Help (search snippet)](https://help.getmaintainx.com/complete-a-work-order-with-maintainx-assist)
- Reviewers describe MaintainX AI as "assistive, not autonomous" — summary of a review aggregator in search results ([Taqtics](https://taqtics.co/reviews/maintainx/), a competitor)

### Inferences
- The most copyable idea for the "Copiloto Taller": the **knowledge-capture loop at WO close**. A 2-minute voice note at closing becomes a structured summary attached to the asset and feeds the RAG. SICOM's Copiloto already uses fleet history, but MaintainX turns closing into a capture point with low friction (voice, mechanics on site), with an "N/A" when information is missing so nothing is invented.
- Second: **admin curation of sources**. Deleting bad summaries from an asset's corpus so the AI stops using them. Useful against "contaminated" history.
- Third: **cross-site knowledge for identical models**. SICOM can do this natively: same make/model (e.g. Volvo, Scania, MB) shares summaries between equipment and faenas.
- Fourth: cheap passive ML features with high value and explicit thresholds: anomaly detection on horómetro/km readings (≥3 history points; decimal errors, negatives, out of range) and time estimates (asset + 1 attribute). SICOM already corrects horómetros by hand (Radicom), so this would prevent errors at entry.
- Fifth: procedure generation from a nameplate photo, PDF or voice. Relevant to the 85 manufacturer pautas SICOM already has as checklists.
- Weak spot: everything generative is Enterprise-only (custom price), and MaintainX does not do predictive maintenance itself (it delegates to Augury/KCF). SICOM, with its own GPS/rFMS, could do basic condition alerts natively.

### Gaps
- MaintainX does not publish which LLM it uses, the supported languages for voice/summaries (e.g. Spanish), whether answers cite the manual page, or usage limits.
- I found no independent evaluations of the accuracy of CoPilot/Assist answers.
- Creating a WO from free text or voice (not just closing one): there are indications ("creates work orders") but I did not see the exact flow documented.

## 3. User opinions: praise, complaints, pricing and feature tiers

### Takeaway
Ratings are very high (around 4.8/5 on Capterra, G2 and Gartner). Praise centers on **ease of use and adoption, the mobile app, QR codes on equipment, and fast support**. Complaints center on **the price jump from Essential to Premium and feature gating by tier, limited customization (fixed priorities, asset hierarchy), reporting depth (people end up in Power BI), crowded dashboards, and PM templates that can't be duplicated**. Caution: most of the critical analyses come from competitor blogs, and I could not read Capterra, Gartner or Reddit directly (403 / not accessed).

### Cited Findings
- Ratings: Capterra about 4.8/5 with about 1,036–1,039 reviews; G2 4.8/5 (~1,512 reviews); Gartner Peer Insights 4.8 (428 ratings) — figures quoted by aggregators in search results ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/179296/GetMaintainx/reviews/), [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/market/enterprise-asset-management-software/vendor/maintainx/product/maintainx/likes-dislikes), [Infraspeak](https://infraspeak.com/en/compare/maintainx-review)). Note: I could not open Capterra or Gartner (HTTP 403).
- Praise: an intuitive interface that eases adoption "even for tech-resistant teams"; a strong mobile app (status, comments and photos in a few taps); support that answers "usually within hours"; testimonial from a small-business Fleet Manager: a "centralized, digital system that documents and reports the full history of each piece of equipment" — [Coast (competitor)](https://coastapp.com/blog/maintainx-review/)
- Reddit (r/IndustrialMaintenance, r/FacilityManagement, quoted by aggregators): the interface is "far better" than older CMMS; operators value the **equipment QR tags**; less customizable than Limble (priorities, conditional formatting) — search result summary citing [Taqtics](https://taqtics.co/?p=10536) and [checkthat.ai](https://checkthat.ai/brands/maintainx/reviews). I did not read the original threads.
- Complaints (competitor source): crowded dashboard for new users; **priorities fixed** at None/Low/Medium/High with no custom labels ("Urgent"); asset hierarchy that doesn't clearly show relationships; **PM templates can't be duplicated**; complex searches need expert knowledge; customization limited for specialized workflows; newer features tend to land in higher tiers — [Coast (competitor)](https://coastapp.com/blog/maintainx-review/)
- The "Essential → Premium jump" is described as the most frequent pricing criticism — summary in search results ([Taqtics](https://taqtics.co/?p=10536)). On Capterra: copying/pasting assets is slow, and it takes cross-department effort to align processes — [Capterra (search snippets)](https://www.capterra.com/p/179296/GetMaintainx/reviews/)
- Advanced reporting takes time to learn — [MaintainX reporting comparison guide](https://www.getmaintainx.com/es/blog/best-maintenance-reporting-software-comparison-guide)
- **Official tiers** (as of Nov 2026), priced per user with a login (requesters are free) — [MaintainX Pricing](https://www.getmaintainx.com/pricing):
  - **Basic (US$0)**: unlimited WOs; 2 active recurring PMs; limited WOs with procedures/images; 1 month of analytics; 1 dashboard.
  - **Essential (US$20/user/month annual; US$25 monthly)**: unlimited PM and images; 3 months of analytics; 3 dashboards.
  - **Premium (US$65 annual; US$75 monthly)**: unlimited analytics; meter-based maintenance; inventory, vendors, POs; **time and cost tracking**; REST API; external WOs for contractors; CSV export; account manager.
  - **Enterprise (custom pricing)**: Report Builder (AI); unlimited dashboards; **MaintainX Assist**; Asset Health Insights; Resource Planning; escalations; IoT; SSO; custom permissions; multi-site with reporting; Global Parts/Procedures. OT connectors and some ERP integrations are charged extra.
- Plans apply to the whole organization (users can't be on different plans) — [MaintainX Help: Pricing explained](https://intercom.help/maintainx/articles/2638859-pricing-explained)

### Inferences
- MaintainX wins on adoption (simple mobile, QR) and loses on configurability and analytical depth unless you pay Enterprise. For SICOM the lesson is that technician UX matters more than KPI breadth. SICOM's advantage is customization (it already has many things MaintainX lacks: configurable priorities, NC → OS flows, faena-specific reports).
- Paying for "cost per asset" or time and cost needs Premium (US$65/user), and AI needs Enterprise. In Chile, with many workshop users, that is expensive, which supports building in-house.

### Gaps
- I could not read G2, Capterra, Gartner or Reddit directly. The opinions come from snippets and aggregators, several of them competitors (Coast, Facilio, Infraspeak, Taqtics, Maintainly) with an obvious bias.
- No user opinions found specifically on CoPilot/Assist (quality, hallucinations).
- Offline mode: the pricing page mentions it but doesn't say which plan includes it, and I did not verify its depth.

## 4. Public case studies (fleet / transport / mining) with measurable results

### Takeaway
There are no mining case studies with hard numbers. The closest to fleet is **Autowash** (24-vehicle service fleet, MTTR −74%, with AI over uploaded manuals). Others with figures are in materials/industry (Titan America −30% unplanned downtime, Interroll −20%). All figures are self-reported by MaintainX and don't isolate the effect of AI.

### Cited Findings
- **Autowash** (car washes, 26 locations in Colorado, 24-vehicle service fleet): uploaded manuals and guides to build the AI knowledge base, technicians look up steps in the middle of a repair (e.g. a first-time pump replacement) without depending on seniors, knowledge is shared across sites. Results "since implementing MaintainX": **MTTR −74%** (>US$3 M in downtime costs avoided), **reactive WOs −15%**, productivity +~5 pp (~US$50k). It does not separate the effect of AI from the rest of the platform and gives no dates — [MaintainX case study Autowash](https://www.getmaintainx.com/case-studies/autowash)
- **Titan America** (construction materials): −30% unplanned downtime — [casestudies.com](https://www.casestudies.com/company/maintainx/case-study/how-titan-america-decreased-unplanned-maintenance-by-30-with-maintainx)
- **Interroll**: −20% unplanned downtime after migrating — [casestudies.com](https://www.casestudies.com/company/maintainx/case-study/interroll-cuts-unplanned-downtime-by-20-after-transitioning-to-maintainx)
- **Redimix**: maintenance spend cut by more than half year over year with a "single source of truth" — [Plant Services](https://www.plantservices.com/department/what-works/article/55388789/case-study-redimix-cuts-year-over-year-maintenance-spend-by-more-than-half-with-a-single-source-of-truth)
- **Southeast Power** (utility fleet): built a fleet PM program from nothing (no PM, SOPs or systems in 2019). I saw no quantified figures in the excerpt — [casestudies.com](https://www.casestudies.com/company/maintainx/case-study/how-southeast-power-launched-a-successful-pm-program-with-maintainx)
- **Navistar** (trucks): parts inventory management case — [casestudies.com](https://www.casestudies.com/company/maintainx/case-study/navistar-customer-case-study)
- **DeLong**: more than 35,000 WOs, all assets moved to PM — [casestudies.com](https://www.casestudies.com/company/maintainx/case-study/with-more-than-35000-work-orders-delong-moves-all-assets-to-preventive-maintenance)
- **Allwest Engineering**: less downtime across 20+ plants — [MaintainX case study](https://www.getmaintainx.com/case-studies/allwest-engineering-maintainx-case-study)
- The mining page claims reduced unplanned downtime but names no customers with figures — [MaintainX Mining](https://www.getmaintainx.com/industries/mining)
- "TransGlobal" (700+ vehicles, −18% maintenance costs) appears only on a consultancy's page — [4Spot Consulting](https://4spotconsulting.com/?p=48602). Possibly an illustrative or unverified case; treat with caution.

### Inferences
- The most cited metric is MTTR, and the AI story (Autowash) ties directly to less dependence on senior mechanics. That is the same value proposition as SICOM's Copiloto Taller. SICOM could measure its own impact on MTTR / repeated questions to rival this claim with its own data.

### Gaps
- No case studies from mining, heavy trucking or equipment rental with audited figures.
- I did not open Titan America, Interroll or Allwest in full (methodology and timeframes unknown).
